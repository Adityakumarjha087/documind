# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and Authentication
All accounts must use the company single sign-on with multi-factor authentication enabled.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must never be reused across services. Hardware
security keys are mandatory for administrators of production systems.

## Network Access
Employees must connect through the corporate VPN when accessing internal systems from outside the
office. Public Wi-Fi networks may only be used together with the VPN.

## Data Classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data is
Restricted and may only be stored in approved systems located in the EU region. Restricted data
must never be pasted into unapproved third-party AI tools.

## Incident Reporting
Suspected security incidents, including lost laptops or phishing emails, must be reported to
security@acme.example within one hour of discovery. The security team runs an on-call rotation
and acknowledges reports within 15 minutes during business hours.

## Device Security
Laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity.
Operating system updates must be installed within seven days of release.
