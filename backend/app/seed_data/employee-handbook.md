# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Working Hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in each employee's local time zone. Outside core hours,
employees may arrange their schedule freely as long as team commitments are met.

## Remote Work
Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period may work remotely up to three days per
week. Fully remote arrangements require approval from a director and the People team. Managers
approve recurring remote schedules in the HR portal.

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off per month, for a total of 21 days per year.
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next calendar year; any remaining days expire on
March 31. Requests longer than five consecutive days should be submitted at least two weeks ahead.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6
weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken within the first year after the child's arrival.

## Expenses
Business travel must be booked through the Acme travel portal. Expense reports must be submitted
within 30 days of the purchase with itemised receipts. The daily meal allowance during business
travel is 60 USD. Economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours.

## Equipment
Every employee receives a laptop that is refreshed every three years. Remote employees receive a
monthly internet stipend of 50 USD and a one-time home office budget of 500 USD.
