# Acme Analytics Product FAQ

## What is Acme Analytics?
Acme Analytics is a self-serve business intelligence platform that connects to data warehouses
such as Snowflake, BigQuery and PostgreSQL and lets teams build dashboards without writing SQL.

## Pricing Plans
The Starter plan costs 29 USD per user per month and includes up to 10 dashboards. The Growth plan
costs 59 USD per user per month and includes unlimited dashboards, scheduled reports and row-level
security. Enterprise pricing is custom and adds SSO, audit logs and a 99.95% uptime SLA.

## Data Refresh
Dashboards refresh every 15 minutes on the Growth plan and every hour on the Starter plan.
Enterprise customers can configure refresh intervals down to one minute.

## Free Trial
New workspaces get a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card is required, and trial
workspaces are downgraded to a read-only state when the trial ends.

## Data Retention and Deletion
Query results are cached for 24 hours. When a customer cancels, their workspace data is deleted
permanently 30 days after cancellation unless they request earlier deletion.
